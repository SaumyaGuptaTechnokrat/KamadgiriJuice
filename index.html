Our Story

A corner stall, an honest cup

Kamad Giri Juice & Shake is the kind of stop you make on the way home — a hand-painted signboard, a steel counter, and someone squeezing your juice while you wait. No shortcuts, no syrup-only "fresh" juice. Just fruit, ice, milk and a kettle of jaggery tea working overtime.

  • Cut & juiced fresh

    Every order squeezed to the glass, not pre-mixed.

  • No syrups, ever

    Real fruit and real ice — that's the whole recipe.

  • Made to order

    Nothing sits waiting — it's poured when you ask for it.

— the fragrance of the nation, in every cup of tea.
